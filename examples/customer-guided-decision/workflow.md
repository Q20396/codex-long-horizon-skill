# Workflow

1. Echo the desired outcome, decision, audience, success criteria, approved
   materials, effects, sensitive-data boundary, and stop condition.
2. Confirm that the two supplied locators are the complete read scope.
3. Assess every material conclusion as `FACT`, `INFERENCE`, or `UNKNOWN`.
4. Choose one status from `READY_FOR_CUSTOMER_DECISION`,
   `MORE_EVIDENCE_NEEDED`, or `BLOCKED`.
5. Produce Layer 1 for the customer without schema, receipt, CI, commit, hash,
   or validator terminology.
6. Produce Layer 2 with the exact operator scope and the fixed pending approval
   state.
7. Produce Layer 3 with source locators, checks, gaps, and limitations.
8. Stop after one next safe action. Do not interpret the outcome as permission
   to inspect more files or implement a change.
